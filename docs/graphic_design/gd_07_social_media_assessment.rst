=================================
Assessment: social media content
=================================

.. admonition:: Task summary

   **Type:** Media production

   **You will:** respond to a client brief by planning and designing a set of three
   social media posts that work together as a campaign.

   **Hand in:** planning sheet, three finished posts (PNG or JPG), the PSD files,
   a mock-up showing the posts on a phone, and a written reflection.

----

Choose a client
---------------

.. tab-set::

   .. tab-item:: Riverbend Community Garden

      **Brief:** The garden wants more young people (12 to 18) to volunteer on
      Saturday mornings.

      **Posts needed:**

      #. A square post introducing the garden and why volunteering is fun
      #. A portrait post with the volunteer days, time and how to sign up
      #. A Story (9 : 16) with a countdown or "this Saturday" message

      **Rules:** Use the Riverbend brand guide in the student kit.

   .. tab-item:: Your school

      **Brief:** Promote an upcoming school event (a sports carnival, production,
      open night, market day or fundraiser).

      **Posts needed:**

      #. A "save the date" post
      #. A post with the full details (what, when, where, cost)
      #. A Story for the day of the event

      **Rules:** Use your school's logo and colours. Ask your teacher for the
      school's brand rules. Do not include photos of students without permission.

   .. tab-item:: A local business

      **Brief:** A real local business (with their permission) or a made-up one
      wants to promote a new product, menu item or service.

      **Posts needed:**

      #. A teaser post that builds excitement
      #. A launch post showing the product and price
      #. A Story with a special offer and a call to action

      **Rules:** Create a simple brand guide first: logo, 3 colours (hex codes),
      2 fonts.

Requirements for all clients
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* Three posts that clearly belong together (**repetition** and **unity**)
* At least **two different sizes** (for example square and Story)
* At least one photo you have **edited** using adjustment layers or a mask
* Clear **hierarchy** and readable text at phone size
* All images, fonts and icons used legally, with a credits list

----

Step 1: Investigate
-------------------

Use ``social_media_planning_sheet.pdf`` from the student kit.

#. **The client:** what do they do? What do they want people to do after seeing
   the posts?
#. **The audience:** age, interests, which platform they use.
#. **Research:** find three posts by similar organisations. For each, note one
   thing that works (use design words) and one thing that does not.
#. **Mood board:** collect colours, fonts and images that match the feel you want.

Step 2: Generate ideas
----------------------

Sketch **at least three** different layout ideas for the first post. Thumbnail
sketches can be small and quick. Label the hierarchy (1st, 2nd, 3rd thing to see).

Step 3: Plan
------------

Choose your best idea and draw a more detailed mock-up for **each** of the three
posts. Write the exact text for each post and your hex colours and fonts.

Step 4: Produce
---------------

Make the posts in Photoshop. Use guides, named layers and non-destructive editing.

Step 5: Mock-up and test
------------------------

#. Put your finished posts onto the phone mock-up template from the kit (place
   each post as a layer and use a **clipping mask** onto the screen shape).
#. Show the mock-ups to **two people from the target audience**. Ask:

   * What is this post about?
   * What should you do after seeing it?
   * What would you change?

#. Write down their answers and make at least one improvement.

Rubric
------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 16 21 21 21 21

   * - Criterion
     - Beginning
     - Developing
     - Proficient
     - Advanced
   * - **Planning** (investigation, sketches, plan)
     - Little planning
     - Some research; one idea sketched
     - Clear audience analysis; three sketched ideas; detailed plan
     - Insightful research and mood board; ideas developed with clear reasons
   * - **Production** (Photoshop skills)
     - Posts are incomplete or messy
     - Posts are complete; some alignment or quality issues
     - Neat, well-aligned posts at correct sizes; edited photo; non-destructive work
     - Polished, professional finish; confident use of masks, styles and adjustments
   * - **Communication** (design principles, audience)
     - Message is unclear
     - Message is clear; some use of design principles
     - Strong hierarchy, contrast and repetition; suits the audience
     - Creative, consistent campaign that would work for the real client
   * - **Evaluation** (testing and reflection)
     - Little or no reflection
     - Describes the posts
     - Uses feedback and design words to explain choices and improvements
     - Critically judges the campaign against the brief with specific evidence

----

Reflection questions
--------------------

.. structuredquestion:: Social media reflection
   :total-marks: 12

   .. subquestion:: Who is your target audience, and how did you design your posts to suit them?
      :marks: 3

      .. model-answer::

         Describes the audience specifically (age, interests, platform) and links
         at least two design choices to them, for example "Teenagers scroll
         quickly, so I used a large bold heading of only four words."

   .. subquestion:: Explain how you used two principles of design in your posts.
      :marks: 3

      .. model-answer::

         Names two principles correctly (such as repetition and hierarchy) and
         gives specific examples from the posts and the effect on the viewer.

   .. subquestion:: What feedback did you get, and what did you change because of it?
      :marks: 3

      .. model-answer::

         Quotes or summarises real feedback, describes the change made and explains
         how the change improved the post.

   .. subquestion:: Explain how you used images and fonts legally and ethically.
      :marks: 3

      .. model-answer::

         Lists sources and licences (such as own photos, CC BY images credited with
         TASL, Google Fonts), and comments on any ethical choices such as getting
         permission for photos of people or not misleading viewers with edits.
