Vocabulary flashcards
---------------------

.. flashcards:: Graphic design words
   :id: y10mm-gd-vocab
   :shuffle:

   # Elements and principles
   Contrast | Making things different (size, colour, weight) so they stand out
   Alignment | Lining things up along an edge or centre
   Repetition | Reusing colours, fonts and shapes so designs belong together
   Proximity | Placing related things close together
   Hierarchy | Showing the most important thing first
   White space | Empty space around and between things
   Balance | Spreading visual weight so a design feels stable
   Rule of thirds | Placing important things on the lines of a 3 × 3 grid

   # Colour
   Complementary | Colours opposite each other on the colour wheel
   Analogous | Colours next to each other on the colour wheel
   RGB | Red, green, blue: the colour model for screens
   CMYK | Cyan, magenta, yellow, black: the colour model for printing
   Hex code | A six-character code for a screen colour, such as #2E7D32
   Tint | A colour with white added

   # Images and law
   Raster | An image made of pixels
   Vector | An image made of points and curves that stays sharp at any size
   Lossy | Compression that throws away some detail, like JPG
   Copyright | The legal right of a creator to control copies of their work
   Creative Commons | Licences that let creators share work with conditions
   Fair dealing | Australian law that allows some use without permission, such as for study

   # Photoshop
   Clipping mask | Shows a layer only inside the shape of the layer below
   Layer mask | Hides parts of a layer: black hides, white shows
   Adjustment layer | A layer that changes colour or brightness without changing pixels
   Non-destructive | Editing that can be changed or undone later

Mixed quiz

.. mcqgroup::
   :show-instant-feedback:
   :shuffle-questions:
   :num-questions: 10

   .. multichoice::

      Which principle makes a set of posts look like they belong together?

      [x] Repetition | Correct.
      [ ] Proximity | Not quite.
      [ ] Balance | Not quite.
      [ ] Emphasis | Not quite.

   .. multichoice::

      Which colour model is used for printing?

      [ ] RGB | RGB is for screens.
      [x] CMYK | Correct.
      [ ] HEX | Hex codes describe screen colours.
      [ ] PNG | That is a file type.

   .. multichoice::

      A logo needs to look sharp on a business card and a billboard. It should be...

      [x] a vector | Correct.
      [ ] a small JPG | It would be blurry when enlarged.
      [ ] a GIF | Only 256 colours and raster.
      [ ] a screenshot | Low quality.

   .. multichoice::

      Which font group has small "feet" on the letters?

      [x] Serif | Correct.
      [ ] Sans serif | Sans means without.
      [ ] Script | Script looks like handwriting.
      [ ] Monospace | Monospace has equal-width letters.

   .. multichoice::

      What does **CC BY-NC** mean?

      [x] Credit the creator and do not use it to make money | Correct.
      [ ] No credit needed | BY means attribution is required.
      [ ] You cannot change it | That is ND.
      [ ] It costs money | Creative Commons is free with conditions.

   .. multichoice::

      Which file type supports transparency but **not** animation?

      [x] PNG | Correct.
      [ ] GIF | GIF supports animation.
      [ ] JPG | JPG does not support transparency.
      [ ] MP4 | That is video.

   .. multichoice::

      The space between lines of text is called...

      [x] leading | Correct.
      [ ] kerning | Kerning is between two letters.
      [ ] tracking | Tracking is across all letters.
      [ ] weight | Weight is thickness.

   .. multichoice::

      What colour is ``#000000``?

      [x] Black | Correct. No red, green or blue light.
      [ ] White | White is #FFFFFF.
      [ ] Grey | Not quite.
      [ ] Red | Red is #FF0000.

   .. multichoice::

      On a layer mask, white...

      [x] reveals the layer | Correct.
      [ ] hides the layer | Black hides.
      [ ] turns the layer white | No.
      [ ] deletes the mask | No.

   .. multichoice::

      Where should the main subject go according to the rule of thirds?

      [x] On the third lines or where they cross | Correct.
      [ ] Dead centre | Not according to the rule.
      [ ] Touching the edge | No.
      [ ] Upside down | No.

   .. multichoice::

      What size is a Story or Reel?

      [x] 1080 × 1920 pixels | Correct.
      [ ] 1080 × 1080 pixels | That is a square post.
      [ ] 1920 × 1080 pixels | That is wide (landscape).
      [ ] 500 × 500 pixels | Too small.

   .. multichoice::

      How long does copyright usually last in Australia?

      [x] 70 years after the creator dies | Correct.
      [ ] 5 years | Much longer than that.
      [ ] Until the work is posted online | Posting does not end copyright.
      [ ] Forever | It does end eventually.
