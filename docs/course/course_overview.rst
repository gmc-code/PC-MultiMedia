==================================
Year 10 Multimedia: course guide
==================================

Welcome to Multimedia. In this subject you will make **animations**, design
**graphics** for social media and build your own **website**. You will also
learn to plan your work, test it and say what you would do better next time.

.. admonition:: What you will be able to do by the end

   * Plan and make an animated GIF that tells a short story or sends a message
   * Design social media graphics for a real client, using a design brief
   * Build a small website with HTML and CSS, and add a little JavaScript
   * Use design words to explain and judge your work and the work of others
   * Use images, music and fonts legally and give credit to their creators

----

How the course is set out
-------------------------

The course has three units. Each semester runs the same three units.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 15 60

   * - Unit
     - Length
     - What you make
   * - :doc:`Animation </animation/anim_index>`
     - 4 weeks
     - An animated **GIF** made in Photoshop from a design brief
   * - :doc:`Graphic design </graphic_design/gd_index>`
     - 4 weeks
     - A set of **social media posts** for a client
   * - :doc:`Web design (HTML and CSS) </web_design/web_index>`
     - 8 weeks
     - A **portfolio website** with several linked pages

Every unit follows the same pattern:

#. **Learn** the ideas. Read the notes and try the quick quizzes.
#. **Practise** the skills. Follow the step-by-step tutorials.
#. **Make** your assessment piece from a design brief.
#. **Reflect** on what worked and what you would change.

How your work is judged
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Each task has a rubric. Your teacher looks at four things:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 75

   * - Criterion
     - What it means
   * - **Planning and design**
     - You plan before you make. Your plan matches the brief.
   * - **Production skills**
     - You use the software well. Your work is neat and finished.
   * - **Communication**
     - Your work suits its audience and sends a clear message.
   * - **Evaluation**
     - You can explain your choices and say how to improve.

The design process
------------------

Designers do not just start making things. They follow a process. You will use
this process in every unit.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 80

   * - Stage
     - What you do
   * - **Investigate**
     - Read the brief. Who is the audience? What does the client want? Look at
       examples made by other people.
   * - **Generate ideas**
     - Sketch several different ideas. Do not stop at your first one.
   * - **Plan**
     - Choose the best idea. Make a storyboard, a mock-up or a wireframe.
   * - **Produce**
     - Make it in the software. Save often.
   * - **Evaluate**
     - Test it. Ask others what they think. Check it against the brief.

.. ordering:: text
   :no-indent:

   Investigate: read the brief and look at examples
   Generate ideas: sketch several different ideas
   Plan: make a storyboard, mock-up or wireframe
   Produce: make it in the software
   Evaluate: test it and check it against the brief

Good habits
-----------

.. tip::

   * **Save early, save often.** Press **Ctrl + S** every few minutes.
   * **Name your files clearly**, for example ``smith_gif_v2.psd``, not ``untitled.psd``.
   * **Keep a folder for each unit** with subfolders for ``planning``, ``working``
     and ``final``.
   * **Back up** your work to your school cloud drive at the end of each lesson.
   * **Write down where you got every image, sound and font.** You will need this
     list for your credits.

Using other people's work
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Almost every photo, song and font online belongs to someone. In Australia work
is protected by **copyright** as soon as it is made. You must only use work that
you made yourself, work you have permission to use, or work with a licence that
allows it (such as **Creative Commons**). You will learn more about this in
:doc:`/graphic_design/gd_04_images_copyright`.
