CHRISTMAS SCENE STUDENT KIT
===========================

index.html          Double-click this to see your scene in a web browser.
my_scene.js         The list of your GIFs. This is the only file you change.
gifs/               Save the GIFs you make in Photoshop here.
images/             The background picture.
photoshop_frames/   Ready-made frames to turn into GIFs in Photoshop.
                      star_1 to star_6 and tree_plain are used in Parts 1 and 3.
                      There is a folder of frames for each of the bell, bauble,
                      present, candle, snowman and gingerbread.
                      There are also folders for the pictures that travel:
                      santa_sleigh, elf_green, elf_red, penguin, snowball
                      and shooting_star.
                      Small GIFs to spawn: gift, note, sparkle.
photoshop_parts/    The separate pieces of each picture, for making your
                    own frames (harder).
engine/             The code that makes the scene work. Do not change it.

Follow the tutorial "Make a clickable Christmas scene" to get started.
