=================
Unit 1: Animation
=================

In this unit you will learn how animation tricks our eyes into seeing movement.
You will make short animated **GIFs** in Adobe Photoshop and finish with your own
GIF made from a design brief.

.. admonition:: Assessment task

   **Animated GIF** (media production). You will plan, make and evaluate a looping
   GIF for a client. See :doc:`anim_06_gif_assessment`.

Four-week plan
--------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 12 44 44

   * - Week
     - Learn
     - Make
   * - 1
     - :doc:`anim_01_what_is_animation` and :doc:`anim_02_principles`
     - Flip book or sticky-note animation
   * - 2
     - :doc:`anim_03_photoshop_timeline`
     - Blinking eyes GIF, then :doc:`anim_04_bouncing_ball`
   * - 3
     - :doc:`anim_05_tweening_banner`
     - Animated banner; start the assessment storyboard
   * - 4
     - :doc:`anim_06_gif_assessment`
     - Finish, export and reflect on your GIF
