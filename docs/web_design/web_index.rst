=================================
Unit 3: Web design (HTML and CSS)
=================================

In this unit you will build real web pages by writing code. You will use
**HTML** for the content and structure, **CSS** for the look, and a little
**JavaScript** to make things happen when people click. You will finish by
designing and building your own portfolio website.

.. admonition:: Assessment task

   **Website portfolio.** Plan, build and test a website of at least four linked
   pages that shows off your work from this course. See
   :doc:`web_11_portfolio_assessment`.

Eight-week plan
---------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 10 50 40

   * - Week
     - Learn
     - Make
   * - 1
     - :doc:`web_01_how_the_web_works` and :doc:`web_02_first_page`
     - Your first web page
   * - 2
     - :doc:`web_03_text_links_images` and :doc:`web_04_lists_tables`
     - A fact page about a topic you like
   * - 3
     - :doc:`web_05_css_basics`
     - Style your fact page
   * - 4
     - :doc:`web_06_box_model` and :doc:`web_07_layout_flexbox`
     - A page with a header, navigation and cards
   * - 5
     - :doc:`web_08_responsive_accessibility`
     - Make your page work on a phone
   * - 6
     - :doc:`web_09_ux_design` and :doc:`web_10_javascript`
     - Wireframes for your portfolio; a dark mode button
   * - 7 and 8
     - :doc:`web_11_portfolio_assessment`
     - Build, test and evaluate your portfolio

Starter kit
-----------

The kit has a folder of starter files for each tutorial, plus a portfolio
template and placeholder images.

:download:`web_student_kit.zip <web_kit/web_student_kit.zip>`

What you need
-------------

* **Visual Studio Code** (VS Code), or the code editor your school uses
* A web browser such as **Chrome**, **Edge** or **Firefox**
* Optional: the **Live Server** extension for VS Code, which refreshes your page
  every time you save
